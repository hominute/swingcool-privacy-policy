SwingCool · 스과방

Privacy Policy

Effective: November 8, 2026

In short: SwingCool collects no personal information. Your practice history and settings stay on your device.

SwingCool is a golf practice timer with no account and no sign-in. It has no ads, and the developer runs no server. The app and 20 themes are free. The only purchase is Theme Plus, an optional one-time purchase that unlocks the Fire Out theme.

01 · Information we collect

We don't collect personal information

We don't receive your name, email, phone number, location, device identifiers or anything like them. There's no account to create. Apart from checking your purchase with the App Store or Google Play, the app doesn't send data to the developer or to any other company.

02 · Data stored on your device

What stays on your device

The data below is stored only on your phone (iPhone or Android) and, if you use one, your watch. The developer can't see it.

03 · Phone and watch

Your phone and watch talk to each other directly

If you use a watch, your routine settings, the state of a running practice and your theme are synced between the phone and the watch. Apple Watch uses Apple's WatchConnectivity. Wear OS watches such as Galaxy Watch use Google's Wear Data Layer.

Both are device-to-device connections provided by the operating system, so nothing goes through a developer server and the developer never receives it.

04 · Ads, purchases, tracking

No ads, no tracking, one optional purchase

The app contains no ad SDK and no analytics or tracking tools. On iOS it never asks for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency).

The only in-app purchase is Theme Plus. Buy it once and the Fire Out theme unlocks. Everything else works without it. Payment is handled by the App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google), and they look after your payment method and account details.

To check whether you own Theme Plus, the app asks the App Store or Google Play on your device and keeps only the answer, owned or not, on the device. The developer receives no payment details or personal information. Apple's or Google's privacy policy covers the payment itself. If you bought Remove Ads in an earlier version, it's recognized the same way and Theme Plus is already yours.

05 · Children

Nothing is collected from children either

SwingCool isn't made for children under 13. Either way, it collects no personal information from anyone, whatever their age. If you have questions, email us below.

06 · Deleting your data

Delete the app and the data goes with it

Because everything lives on the device, deleting the app deletes your history and settings. The developer keeps no copy.

Your Theme Plus purchase stays with your Apple or Google account, so after reinstalling the app you can get it back with Restore purchase.

If iCloud Backup is on for your iPhone, app data may be included in that backup. You manage it in your Apple account settings.
